# Templatformer

3D platformer in the vein of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (lock-on, item-gated dungeons) and Sonic (momentum, slopes, speed). Godot 4.5, GDScript, Compatibility renderer so it runs on the web.

## Commands

- `bash tools/setup_godot.sh` installs Godot to `~/.local/bin/godot` and imports the project. The SessionStart hook runs it.
- `bash tools/test.sh` runs the headless feel tests (`tests/run_tests.gd`). Run it before every push.
- `bash tools/export_web.sh` builds `build/web/`. The first run downloads the 1.3 GB template bundle and keeps only the web templates.
- CI (`.github/workflows/web.yml`) runs the tests and export on every PR and deploys `main` to GitHub Pages.

## How the code is laid out

- `scripts/player.gd` is the momentum controller. Speed follows the surface: slopes add or remove speed. It also holds the moves:
  - Mario jump chain: re-jump within `jump_combo_window` of landing while running for a double, then a triple (needs `triple_min_speed`).
  - Wall slide and wall jump: push into a wall in the air to slide, press jump to kick off.
  - Roll (i-frames for `roll_invuln`) comes only from a quick stick tap while holding lock-on (out of neutral and back within `dodge_tap_time`); there is no roll button, and without lock-on a tap just steps (jovi: rolling on every tap made positioning tedious). Stick left/right gives a side hop, back a backflip.
  - Speed above top speed bleeds back at `overspeed_decay` on flat ground only (not for `boost_hold` after a boost pad); slopes act by physics, so running downhill builds speed. Use that in level design (runways can be downhills).
  - Size (`set_small`) changes only on `SizePad`s (shrink and grow pads); there is no shrink item. Small: radius x`small_scale`, slower, lower jumps, and grates, fences and bars (layers 2 and 3) don't stop you. Growing needs room (fails inside a grate). Small with the magnet, `_magnet_line` flies you along an iron's line instead of moving the iron.
  - Holding lock-on with nothing to target is a strafe: facing is fixed and speed is capped at `strafe_speed`.
  - `launch(v)` is how launch pads and other launchers throw you; `air_lock` stops floor snapping from eating the launch.
- `scripts/camera_rig.gd` is the orbit camera. It recenters behind your motion and frames the target while you're locked on, but holds still when you're within 2 m of the target horizontally (bouncing off it). A locked-on homing attack moves the lock to what it homes on, so chains don't swing the camera back.
- `scripts/tuning.gd` holds every feel number. New feel values go here and into `Tuning.EDITABLE` so they show up in the live panel.
- `scripts/level.gd` builds the test room in code. Tests start from positions it records in `marks`.
- Health is in half hearts (`Player.max_hp` 6 = 3 hearts). Anything that can be damaged is in group `hurtable` and has `hurt(amount, from_pos)`. That's the player, monsters, and whatever bombs and the poleaxe hit. The player's is `hurt(amount, from, by = null, heavy = false)`: pass the attacker so the shield can parry it (`by.parried()`), reflect it (`Arrow`) or impale it, and `heavy` for hits that push a block far and break it against a wall.
- `scripts/inventory.gd` holds item definitions (`ITEMS`), counts and the three quick slots (keys 1-3, like Ocarina's C buttons). The poleaxe (id `poleaxe`, "Poleaxe & Shield") is equipment: owning it enables the attack and guard buttons. New items go in `ITEMS` and in the `match` in `use()`.
- `scripts/poleaxe.gd` is the player's one weapon (combat sketchbook VII, jovi 2026-09-30): one attack button, and what you're doing picks the head (no switch button, no extra buttons). `MOVES`: thrust, thrust2, sweep (the combo; the sweep launches), run (running blade above `run_attack_speed`, +1 above `fast_blade_speed`), hammer, spin, air, bash (attack while guarding), flash (full focus). Holding attack freezes a swing at the end of its startup (`frozen`); letting go after `hammer_hold` gives the hammer, after `spin_charge_time` the spin (ground or air). Sweet spots: tip x2 (`TIP_FROM`), sweep edge +1, hammer centre +2. The weapon is `Poleaxe.REACH` (2) times its first size (jovi, 2026-10-01): every range and hit shape (not the shield bash) and the mesh scale with it, so a thrust reaches 6.2 m and the tip starts at 4.5 m; `focus_near`/`focus_far` (6..11 m) sit just outside that reach. A connected swing can be cancelled into the next; no whiff penalty. Hits go through `Monster.strike(amount, from, info)`.
- The shield (`guard` action: C, pad right trigger; tests use `ai_guard`) lives in `player.gd`: block, perfect guard (`perfect_guard`, sets `parry_t`), guard break against a wall, brace (`brace_time` still) that impales charges over `impale_speed`, shield surf (`_surf_step`, landing holding guard) and surf pogo (`_surf_pogo`). No stamina and no vault (jovi). Focus (`focus_meter`) fills while locked on `focus_near`..`focus_far` from a monster; full, attack is a flash step.
- `scripts/bomb.gd`: bombs aren't an item (jovi). The context button next to a `BombFlower` (`scripts/bomb_flower.gd`, regrows in `bomb_regrow`) pulls one out overhead with the fuse lit; context again throws it if you're moving, or sets it down if you're standing still. Powers book (jovi): throws are short (`bomb_throw_speed`, `bomb_throw_keep`) and a bomb stops fast on the ground (`bomb_friction`); bombs never collide with you. A poleaxe hit shape reaching a grounded bomb bats it (`bomb_bat_speed`), and one flying over `BAT_FUSE_SPEED` goes off on the first monster it hits. A pound onto a bomb is a bomb jump (`bomb_jump_speed` up, unhurt). Blasts reach monsters as `strike(.., {"blast": true, "stagger": true})`.
- The context button (`context` action: E, gamepad B; `Player.context()`, tests use `ai_context`) picks up, pulls, plants and puts down (seeds, bombs, pots). It's the one button for carried things.
- `scripts/lighting.gd` makes light a rule. `Lighting.is_lit(point)` is true if a shining node in group `light_sources` (the player wearing the candle hat, lit braziers, burning grass or vines) is within reach with a clear line on collision layer 1, or the sun (group `sun`) has a clear line. Bodies block light, so the player casts a shadow unless they're the source. `Lighting.spread_heat` warms nodes in group `flammable` (`Burnable` grass/vines, `Brazier`, `IceBlock`, `Crate`, wine `Puddle`s, honey `Coat`s). Water douses: `Burnable.douse` and `Brazier.put_out` (wet grass won't catch for `wet_time`; the candle lights a doused brazier again). Burning grass spreads only after `fire_spread_delay`, so lay it in 1 m patches (`Burnable.field`) and the fire visibly walks; it burns monsters standing in it (1 per `fire_damage_every`) and never the player (jovi: your fire attacks are safe).
- `scripts/umbra.gd` is the mirrored ghost from the Umbra item. It appears 1.5 m beside you on the mirror line, or short of a wall. It reads `player.last_wish` and mirrors it across an axis snapped from the camera's right. Dark: floats at its height. Lit: falls and crawls at `umbra_crawl_speed`. Once light knocks it out of the air it falls until it lands. It's on collision layer 4, so it doesn't block the player or light, and only `MoonPlate` sees it. It copies poleaxe moves (`Poleaxe.move_started`) with a greatsword while dark. It mirrors your stick, not your motion, and has a collision exception with you, so pushing into a wall still steers it. Its blade strikes with `{"twin": true}`: the only thing that hurts a `Shade` in the dark. You and it hitting one monster within `pincer_window` is a pincer (`Umbra.pincer`, called from `Monster.strike`): +`pincer_bonus`.
- `scripts/shadow_hall.gd` builds the Ember & Umbra wing (candle room, dark chasm, lantern chasm). Its marks start with `hall_`. `Gate` opens from a brazier's `lit_up` or a moon plate's `pressed`.
- `scripts/iron_cube.gd`, `battery.gd`, `power.gd` and `pad.gd` are Lodestone. Iron blocks are 2 m wide and 4.5 m tall (`IronCube.H`), on a 2 m grid. Each moves a cell at a time when the player's magnet (`magnet_push`: false pulls, true pushes) is in its row or column band, within `magnet_range`, with a clear ray. `Power` (one node in the level) floods power each frame from `power_source` through `conductor` iron to `power_sink` (a `Gate` after `wire()`, or a `Pad` made with `needs_power`). Touching means face to face (`Power.touching`), never corner to corner; a box resting on another (iron on iron sunk in a pit) touches it too. Monsters don't stop a sliding block: `_plough` knocks the one at its leading face on at `iron_plough_knock`.
  - Iron pushed into a pit as deep as it is tall drops in, settles flush (`IronCube.sunk`) and stays as floor: you walk on it and iron slides over it. Into anything deeper it goes back `home`.
  - A powered `Gate` that loses power stays open (`held`) while iron, you, the spider or a monster is in the doorway (`Gate.occupied()`), and closes once it's clear.
- `scripts/lodestone_yard.gd` builds the yard. `LodestoneYard.cell(i, j)` gives cell centres. Its marks start with `yard_`.
- `scripts/scale_garden.gd` builds the Scale garden (marks start with `garden_`). Its pieces: `SizePad`, `Grate` (collision layer 3: stops you at normal size only), `Water` (has a `current` that carries floating crates), `Fan` (wind lanes that carry you only when small), `CrackedFloor`, `CrackedWall` (a normal-size roll or a bomb bursts it). The ground slab is cut around `ScaleGarden.GROUND_HOLES`; `ScaleGarden.basin` lines a hole.
- Collision layers: 1 world (blocks light), 2 bars and railings (stop you at normal size, not light or Umbra), 3 grates, 4 Umbra, 5 props (seed cubes, pots, the spider), 6 spider mesh (stops only the spider: the gear cage).
- `scripts/plants.gd` keeps every plant congruent (jovi, 2026-10-01): `Plants.mark(node, kind)` puts it in `plants`, `plant_<kind>` and its kind's groups. Vines (`Burnable` "vines"): `climbable`, `lash_posts`, `flammable`. Wood (a seed's trunk and roots): `climbable`, `lash_posts`. Grass (`Burnable` "grass"): `flammable`. Anything new that grows calls it; don't add those groups by hand. `Burnable.make(..., regrow)` grows burnt ones back (the moves yard's climbing vines, 20 s). Bomb flowers and the colossus's fur aren't in the rules (fur is climbable only).
- Carryable things are in group `carryable` with `hold(by)`, `set_down(pos)`, `throw(v)` and optionally `carry_size()` and `loose()`: seeds, bombs, pots and the player. `by` is the player or the spider (anything with `radius()`).
- Level pads are `Pad`s. `level.launch_pad` / `level.boost_pad` make always-on ones.
- `scripts/monster.gd` holds every enemy kind (`Monster.spawn(parent, pos, kind, spiked)`): blob, wolf (circles, bites from outside your front or after `WOLF_PATIENCE`), rusher (heavy charge, crashes into walls), archer (`scripts/arrow.gd`), shield (front shield: point glances, blade cracks, hammer or bash smashes) and brute (armoured: only the hammer or an opening staggers it). They're in groups `monsters`, `targets` (lock-on) and `hurtable`, and recoil after touching you. Attacks go windup, attack, recover: a hit in the windup counters (x1.5), in a missed attack's recovery punishes (+1). Launched monsters juggle (lift 8, 6, 4, then only a spike), spiked ones burst on landing; heavy knockback splats them on walls and bowls them into each other. `spiked` means spikes on its head: pound and homing onto it hurt you (homing skips it), and only a shield surf pogos off it (jovi).
- Powers-book kinds are subclasses of `Monster` (spawn them with `PowersYard.foe(parent, kind, pos)`): `Shade` (`scripts/shade.gd`, a blob that only Umbra's greatsword hurts unless it's lit; exposed it's solid and slow; it won't step into light that isn't yours), `IronKnight` (`scripts/iron_knight.gd`, a shield monster your magnet moves when it's within `knight_magnet_range` in front of you: pull slides it to you with its shield down for `knight_open_time`, push blows it away at `knight_push_knock`) and `PlatedBrute` (`scripts/plated_brute.gd`, two plates; only a blast cracks one, everything else clanks).
- `scripts/combat_yard.gd` builds the combat yard (marks `combat_`, x -116..-58, z -64..-14): downhill into a pack, a spiked row, wolves among pillars, a wall court. Its `Station`s (group `spawners`) respawn their monsters while you're near; tests free them.
- `scripts/powers_yard.gd` builds the Powers yard (Power Combat Sketchbook II, jovi 2026-10-01; marks `power_`, x 120..159, z -43..43, its own slab east of the ground): a strip along x 120..126 with each room's item at its door, then four rooms: shades with Umbra (dark, roofed at 6 m), iron knights among iron blocks, grass fire with shades (dark, 1 m grass patches) and a plated brute with bomb flowers. Its `Squad`s (group `spawners`) respawn like `Station`s. The book's Big Hunt is on hold (jovi): don't build it.
- `scripts/dev_menu.gd` holds the playtest tools, which pause the game:
  - G opens the warp and debug menu (`WARPS` names level marks; give every item, god mode, heal, shrink).
  - P drops a feedback pin: jovi types a note and gets a link with `#pin=CODE`.
  - O loads a pasted pin. The web build loads `#pin=` from the address on start; desktop takes `-- --pin=CODE`.
  - `Pin` (`scripts/pin.gd`) captures and restores position, facing, camera, size, magnet, hearts, items and slots. A code is URL-safe base64 JSON, so decode it (`base64 -d` after swapping `-_` for `+/`) to read jovi's note and spot, and start a test there.
- `scripts/level_rules.gd` checks built areas (`LevelRules.AREAS`) against the level feel rules: ledges under 2 m, gaps under 6 m, 10-12 m gaps without a 12 m runway or a pad nearby, and 3 m ledges as the norm. The first tests run it on every area. Add each new wing's box to `AREAS`, and stop a roofed wing's box under its roof.
- Rootworks (`scripts/rootworks.gd`, marks start with `root_`) holds Winch and Propagule:
  - `Seed` (Propagule) is a 2 m cube (`Seed.SIZE`) on layer 5 (props), which the player, spider and cubes collide with. Carry one (hands full: no poleaxe, no lash, but you can still climb), set it down on soil, mud or roots (groups `soil`, `mud`, `roots`) to plant, or drop it `spear_drop` onto mud. Planted, the cube stays as a stump and grows a trunk from its top (groups `trunks`, `climbable`, `lash_posts`) and grid-axis roots until blocked. Context next to a planted one uproots it, and other plants' roots that its parts blocked grow on (`Seed.regrow_roots`, up to `root_length`). Let go of a seed while climbing and it drops behind you, so 3 m up a vine over mud it spears in. There is no glide (jovi).
  - The Lash item (`Player.use_lash`) is Winch's leash plus Propagule's lash. It grapples to `lash_posts`, fetches loose seeds, stings hurtables, and hooks the spider on a taut rope.
  - The Clockwork Spider (`Spider`) is Winch's walker and the other main character (jovi). It's small (jovi, 2026-10-01): `Spider.RADIUS` 0.175, the small player's size, so bars and grates (layers 2 and 3) don't stop it; only the spider mesh (layer 6) does. The spider item swaps who you steer (`Player.pilot`); piloted, inputs go to `Spider.wish` and the context button to `Spider.context()`, which picks up anything `carryable` beside it (seeds, bombs, and you: `Player.carried_by`) and throws it (moving) or sets it down (still; a seed on soil, mud or roots plants). Carrying, it's as big as its load, so bars and grates stop it again. Unhooked, it breaks past `spider_break` from you. It turns gears it walks past (`Gear._physics_process`).
  - Rootworks' newer spots: the spider cage (plateau A's north-east corner, marks `root_cage_*`: a seed behind a grate, soil at the chasm edge, only the spider gets in) and the vine shelf (east of the ramp, marks `root_vine*`: climb 6 m of vines holding the seed, plant it on the soil up top, cube and ledge grab to the 4.5 m pillar's heart; mud at the foot for spear drops).
  - `Tether` is the taut rope from you (a, the reel) to the spider (b). It bends only around layer 1 and props, finding corners by halving between last frame's end and this frame's; a bend lets go when the rope would turn the other way there. At `max_len` the steered end (`lead`) drags the other by sliding it. A gear with a bend on its rim turns by the rope slid over it. `Gear` drives a lift by `gear_ratio` and keeps its angle.
  - Machinery (`scripts/machinery.gd`, one node in the level): `Gear.turn` only asks; each frame `Machinery` turns every train once, after the spider and the rope. Gears whose rims touch mesh and turn opposite ways; a gear beside a `Rack`'s toothed side slides it a metre per metre of rim; `Machinery.link` gears a crank to a rack (`crank_ratio`). A train asked to turn two ways at once (an odd ring, or the spider passing two meshed gears on the same face) jams (`Gear.jams`, sparks); otherwise it turns as far as its tightest stop allows. Turns are metres of rim; `Gear.STEP` (a quarter turn of a 1 m gear) is a notch. `Gear.cog` makes a free gear (no lift, no stops).
  - `Rack` (`scripts/rack.gd`): a toothed rail flush with the floor sliding `offset` 0..`travel` along `axis`. It carries its `riders` (gears, posts); you and the spider ride it as a moving floor, but standing on it pins it (`Rack.pinned`). A gear riding a rack counts only the spider's own steps (it measures from itself).
  - `ArmGear` (`scripts/arm_gear.gd`): a gear carrying a bridge arm that points one of four ways, a quarter turn per notch (it settles on the nearest notch). `Screw` (`scripts/screw.gd`): a post on a threaded gear that rises `notch_h` per positive notch, flush (floor) to `top`; it lifts what stands on it.
- `scripts/works.gd` builds the Works (marks `works_`, x 159..244 and z -44..16 east of the Powers yard, plus an island at x 258..266): the iron room (`Works.cell(i, j)`: iron fills a pit, then a second iron powers a door and is pulled through it, held open, to a launch pad), the rack ferry (the spider walks round a crank riding the deck rack to cross a 14 m pit), the arm bridge (a notch swings a 17 m bridge across a 14 m chasm), the screws (two flush screws wound to 2 m and 4 m are steps up a 6 m ledge) and the gear train (a flush screw on the input only rises; crank the clutch rack so two gears, not one, sit between input and gate gear). The iron room is left out of `LevelRules.AREAS`: its 2 m pit is for iron.
- Honey & Wine (the Cellar). Liquid flow is kept simple (jovi, 2026-10-01), exactly three behaviours: a spill is one fixed-size puddle on flat ground (`Liquids.RADIUS`; a second spill on it refreshes it, never spreads it), liquid poured on a designer-placed `Runnel` (`scripts/runnel.gd`) runs down it and puddles only at its low end, and one pot fills a planned `Basin` (`scripts/basin.gd`) to the brim (water 1 m deep or more becomes `Water`). No volume spreading or flow sim; don't add one without asking.
  - `scripts/liquids.gd` (static) holds the rules: `spill`, `land`, `under` (honey beats wine beats water; doused wine counts as water), `source_at`, and the hooks every coatable body calls: `frozen_step` (Monster, Spider, Umbra; rock candy only falls), `creature_after`, `player_wish` (drunk sway, the water pipe puts the candle out), `player_drag` (honey: `honey_slow`, jumps `honey_jump`, small you're stuck), `monster_think` (drunk wander, honey lures blobs and wolves, wine lures brutes and rushers within `lure_range`, a `leash` meta keeps it near home) and `monster_after` (honey slows, sober monsters stop at drops). `scripts/coat.gd` (`Coat.of/on`) is each body's state: honey, rock candy (honey plus `candy_heat_time` of heat within `heat_reach`; candy is in `candy` and `targets`), drunk. Water washes a coat off. The player is never coated.
  - `scripts/pot.gd`: carryable (context button), fills while held under a `Spout` (`scripts/spout.gd`: hive, cask, pipe) or in a pool, thrown at `pot_throw_speed` + `pot_throw_run` x your speed, up `pot_throw_up`; it smashes on first touch (or a poleaxe hit or bomb) and spills. `PotShelf` (`scripts/pot_shelf.gd`) puts out a new one after `pot_respawn`.
  - `scripts/puddle.gd`: water dries after `wet_time` and wets what it covers; wine makes you drunk (`drunk_time`, monsters `drunk_monster_time`) and burns for `wine_burn` when lit (a burning trough always shoves the player out); honey holds. `Puddle.pool` is a permanent one.
  - Swap Charm (item `swap`, `Liquids.swap`): you and the rock candy you target (or the nearest ahead, within `swap_range`, with a clear line) trade places; each keeps its velocity.
  - `scripts/crate.gd`: 2 m wooden crate, pushed at `crate_push_speed`, blocks light, burns away in `crate_burn` (water saves it), smashes on a poleaxe hit, floats in `Water` and rides its `current`, carrying you. It goes home when lost. Crates aren't carryable.
  - `scripts/cellar.gd` builds the Cellar (marks `cellar_`, x 136..172, z -150..-102, roofed at 8 m, warp only): candy trap (honey a blob by a brazier across a 13 m pit, swap across), last call (wine on the bridge drunks the leashed brute off it), raft (one pot fills the channel, the crate floats you over) and fire door (douse the burning wine trough; the runnel shows flow).
- `scripts/moves_yard.gd` (marks `moves_`) shows the acclaimed moves in `player.gd`:
  - Climbing: push into anything in group `climbable` (vines, trunks), no grip limit (jovi), holding a seed too (not a lit bomb). `_climb_step` vaults at the top.
  - Ledge grab: falling against a wall with its top within `ledge_reach` hangs you (`hang`). Iron (`IronCube`) can't be grabbed, so it still needs a triple jump.
  - Grind rails (`scripts/rail.gd`, group `rails`, no collision): gravity along the rail, jump hops off.
  - Homing attack: air attack while holding lock-on homes on the nearest `hurtable` or `pogo` node ahead (not spiked monsters), hits it once (it shares the poleaxe's hit list) and bounces you straight up off its top. It picks from every candidate, not just the lock-on target (jovi: sticking to the target broke chains). `Spikes` (group `pogo`) hurt on touch.
  - Ground pound (`_pound_step`): the air attack without lock-on, hands empty, poleaxe not needed (jovi, 2026-09-30). The context button no longer pounds: onto `hurtable`/`pogo` it hits and bounces (homing can chain after); flat landing opens `pound_land` for a high jump, or rolls out if you hold a direction; onto a slope it becomes downhill speed. Jumping out of a ground roll is a long jump; landing it keeps only `long_jump_keep` of the overspeed (`long_jumping`). A pound started within 0.5 s of a ledge pull-up (`_ledge_up_t`) rolls out into a `ledge_long_mult` long jump (jovi liked that chain). Rolls keep `roll_speed_now` and gain it downhill. A wall kick leaves `jump_chain` at 0, so the next landing jump is a double.
  - Perfect dodge: a hit during a roll calls `Hitfx.slow`; `Hitfx.world` slows monsters (they multiply their dt by it).

## Level feel rules (jovi, 2026-09-29)

- Gaps: 6 and 8 m feel nice. Under 6 m is pointless and tedious. 10 m is good because it needs speed and build-up. Anything bigger needs a runway long enough to build it up.
- Heights: under 2 m is pointless. 3 m is situational and slows play, so don't make it the norm.

## Working rules

- Don't spend long proving a level has only one solution (jovi, 2026-09-29). Check the intended route and the obvious skips. jovi and playtesters report cheese.
- Nobody can feel the game from here. Every change to feel ships with a test in `tests/run_tests.gd` that states the number it guarantees (jump height, clearable gap, launch height), plus the web build for the owner to play.
- Tests drive the player with `ai`, `ai_move`, `ai_jump`, `ai_target`, `ai_attack`, `ai_attack_held`, `ai_guard` and `ai_item`, never with real input. Combat tests use `_dummy` (a monster that holds still) and `_hold_attack`. Rolls are a quick `ai_move` tap (the test helper `_tap`). While locked on, give `ai_move` relative to the player's facing, the way a real stick is read relative to the camera.
- Use `absf`, `maxf` and typed vars. Godot's parser rejects `:=` on Variant-typed expressions.
- Keep the web build working: no threads, no GDExtension.
